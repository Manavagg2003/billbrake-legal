Privacy Policy — BillBrake

App: BillBrake: Sub & Trial Alerts (com.antigravity.billbrake) • Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Our Core Privacy Promise

BillBrake is a 100% offline, privacy-first Android application. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any of your personal or financial data.

  • No Bank Logins: BillBrake never asks for bank credentials, credit cards, or third-party bank-sync aggregators.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to sign up or provide an email address.
  • On-Device Storage Only: All subscriptions, free-trial alerts, audit tags, and savings records are stored locally on your Android device in an on-device SQLite database.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

BillBrake collects zero user data: no personal identifiers, no financial data, no location, no SMS/contacts, no advertising IDs, and no third-party behavioral tracking or ads.

3. Android Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional, Android 13+): Used solely to show local renewal and free-trial alerts scheduled on your device. You may deny or revoke this permission anytime in Android Settings.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional upgrades to BillBrake Pro (Monthly, Annual, or Lifetime Unlock) are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment processing under Google’s Privacy Policy. BillBrake receives only a local entitlement confirmation to unlock Pro features and never accesses your payment card information.

5. Backups & Deleting Your Data

Optional CSV/JSON exports are saved only where you choose on your device. You can permanently delete all app data anytime via Android Settings → Apps → BillBrake → Storage → Clear storage or by uninstalling BillBrake.

6. Children’s Privacy

BillBrake is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

7. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email support@billbrake.app.
